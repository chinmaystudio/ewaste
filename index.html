Dispose responsibly

E-waste recycling guide

Different electronics need different handling. Follow local rules and use suitable collection or authorized recycling channels.

📱

Phones & laptops

Back up data, sign out of accounts, and securely erase personal information before reuse or recycling. Remove accessories where appropriate.

🔋

Batteries

Keep batteries separate from general waste. Do not crush, puncture, or burn them. For swollen or damaged batteries, avoid unnecessary handling and seek specialist guidance.

🔌

Cables & accessories

Keep cables dry and bundle them safely. Separate reusable accessories from broken items for suitable reuse or processing.

🛠️

Repair & refurbish

A working or repairable device may have a longer useful life. Consider repair or donation through a trusted service before recycling.

🏭

Authorized recycling

Use appropriate collection channels or authorized e-waste recyclers. Do not dismantle hazardous components without suitable training.

🔄

Circular economy

Reuse, repair, refurbishment, and material recovery keep resources in circulation and reduce demand for new raw materials.

🌱
EcoTech's circular economy promise

Track items through collection, prioritize reuse where appropriate, and record recycling only after it is confirmed. The dashboard reports application activity, not independently verified environmental savings.
